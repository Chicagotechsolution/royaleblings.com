How a collection is built

Designed as a family. Every collection starts as one silhouette study — a curve, a line, a cut — and every piece in it inherits that study. That's why Vesper pieces layer with Vesper, and why nothing here is a one-off.

Released as drops. Each piece is made in a small, stated quantity per drop. When a drop sells through, the piece waits for the next making — we don't backorder what doesn't exist.

Named for what they are. Metals and stones are listed plainly on every piece at release: karat, weight, stone type and origin of grading. Lab-grown is labeled lab-grown.
